Game Rules

1. Voice Channel Requirement

You must always be in your designated VC when roleplaying. If you are asked to join a certain VC for roleplay purposes, you must join.

2. No Random Deathmatch (RDM)

You cannot kill or damage other players without a valid reason. You cannot shoot randomly at vehicles, objects, or in the air without a valid reason.

3. No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

You cannot hit other players, objects, structures, or vehicles, without a valid reason.

4. Realistic Actions

You cannot do actions that cannot realistically be completed. For example: Jumping over a moving vehicle.

5. Traffic Stop Protocol

Minor traffic violations may not be used to create or escalate a priority scene. Do not turn routine traffic stops into chases, shootouts, or hostage situations solely because of the original offense.

6. Value Your Life

You must value your life and safety. You may not put yourself in excessive danger without a very convincing reason. If threatened, you must comply with orders.

7. Character Death & Memory

If you die, your roleplay character must forget all information from its past life and be a new character. You cannot return to the death scene until it has finished.

8. Character Resurrection

When your character dies, roleplay as one of your other approved characters. Every SSU, your dead character is resurrected but will not remember the death cause.

9. No Crime Baiting

You must not commit crimes to attract law enforcement or commit crimes knowing that Law Enforcement Personnel are watching you.

10. Traffic Laws

You must follow all traffic laws unless you have a valid reason to break them. You must drive realistically and cannot D-Sync vehicles on purpose.

11. Crash Roleplay

You must roleplay all crashes unless you were VDMed or D-Synced. Vehicles get disabled if the crash is 60mph+. Both parties must agree to void a 2-party crash.

12. Avatar Realism

Your avatar must not clip through or have unrealistic accessories/animations. Your avatar must be realistically suited to your current roleplay.

13. No 3rd Party Drama

It is prohibited that any drama from a 3rd party community/company is introduced and talked about within Pinal County Roleplay.

14. Animal Roleplay

You may not dress up/act as an animal. The only exception is Police K9 roleplay.

15. No Evading Staff

Evading Staff is prohibited. This includes leaving the game to avoid punishment (LTAP) or resetting in a roleplay scenario without notifying.

16. No Metagaming

You must not use out-of-roleplay information. You cannot use unfair advantages to gain information for better advantage.

17. Scene Joining

You cannot join a scene or force yourself into a scene if you were not originally a part of it.

18. Group Limits

Do not go around the game in groups of more than three players. Mafias can have 5 members at a time. Whitelisted Mafias must follow their specific rules.

19. Robbery Requirements

You must have at least two people to rob the Jewellery Store and at least three people to rob the bank. Wait at least 15 minutes before starting another robbery.

20. Peace Timer

The Peace Timer stops chaos within the server. Refrain from starting priority calls for Emergency Services. Actions that make you wanted risk moderation.

21. Bank Robbery Permissions

You must get permission from staff for a bank robbery scene. You cannot hold FD/EMS or DOT hostage. Self-harm and Terroristic Attacks are not allowed.

22. Appropriate Liveries

Use the appropriate liveries and uniforms provided by Pinal County Roleplay. Do not use ER:LC liveries and uniforms.

23. Traffic Stop Positioning

Pull over to the utmost right side, parallel to the slowest lane with enough space for Law Enforcement to make an interaction.

24. Official Law Enforcement Only

Do not roleplay as Law Enforcement Agencies without their own livery and uniform in the game (ICE, Border Patrol, US Marines, etc.).

25. Safe Zones

Safe zones are locations where no hostile actions may take place: Civilian Spawn, DOT Station, Fire Department, Police Department, Gun Store, and Sheriff's Office.
